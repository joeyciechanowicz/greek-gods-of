# The Little Things the Gods Were For

The big domains (Zeus: the sky, Poseidon: the sea) are only part of it. The Greeks worshipped each
god under many **epithets**, which were titles attached to a particular job, place or cult, and they
had minor gods and *daimones* (spirits) for very specific things. This page collects the small,
specific and odd ones.

Most of these come from local cults recorded by writers such as Pausanias, Athenaeus and Hesychius, or
from personifications in Hesiod and the poets. They were local practices, not universal ones.

---

## The Olympians' smaller jobs

### Zeus

| Epithet | Small thing he was for |
| --- | --- |
| Zeus Ktesios | Household possessions and the family storeroom. He was kept in a jar in the pantry to guard the family's goods |
| Zeus Herkeios | The courtyard and fence of the house; the family altar |
| Zeus Xenios | Hospitality: guests, hosts and strangers |
| Zeus Hikesios | Suppliants (people begging for protection) |
| Zeus Horkios | Oaths |
| Zeus Philios | Friendship |
| Zeus Phratrios | Clans and brotherhoods (*phratries*) |
| Zeus Agoraios | The marketplace and public assemblies |
| Zeus Ombrios | Rain |
| Zeus Apomyios | Keeping **flies** away from sacrifices, especially at Olympia |

### Athena

| Epithet / attribution | Small thing she was for |
| --- | --- |
| Athena Ergane | Craftsmen and workers, especially **weaving and spinning** |
| Potters' patron | Pottery. A Homeric poem calls on her to hold her hand over the kiln so the pots fire well |
| Athena Hippia | Taming horses. She was credited with inventing the **bridle** |
| Inventor of the aulos | The double flute. She invented it, then threw it away because playing it puffed out her cheeks |
| Athena Salpinx | The **trumpet** (at Argos) |
| Shipbuilding | Carpentry and ships; she helped build the *Argo* |
| The olive tree | Her gift to Athens |
| Athena Phratria | Family clans and lineages |
| Athena Ophthalmitis | **Eyes** (at Sparta, where Lycurgus dedicated a temple to her after losing an eye) |
| Athena Hygieia | Health |
| Athena Polias | Guardian of the city |

### Hermes

| Epithet / attribution | Small thing he was for |
| --- | --- |
| *Hermaion* | **Lucky finds**. A windfall found in the road was a "gift of Hermes" |
| Boundary stones & cairns | Boundaries, roads and roadside markers (*herms*) |
| Sleep and dreams | The last libation of the evening went to Hermes before bed |
| Hermes Enagonios | Athletic contests and the gymnasium |
| Hermes Kriophoros | Shepherds; carries a ram on his shoulders |
| Inventor | The **lyre** (from a tortoise shell) and fire-sticks for making fire |
| Heralds | Heralds, messengers and diplomacy |
| Trickery | Liars, cheats and thieves |

### Apollo

| Epithet | Small thing he was for |
| --- | --- |
| Apollo Smintheus | **Mice**: sending plagues of them and getting rid of them |
| Apollo Parnopios | Driving away **locusts** |
| Apollo Lykeios | **Wolves** |
| Apollo Agyieus | Streets and **doorways**; a pillar stood outside the front door |
| Apollo Archegetes | Founding colonies |
| Apollo Nomios | Herdsmen and pastures |
| Apollo Alexikakos | Averting evil and disease |

### Artemis

| Epithet / attribution | Small thing she was for |
| --- | --- |
| Artemis Lochia | Women in labour |
| Artemis Kourotrophos | Nursing and raising young children |
| Brauronia | Young girls before marriage. Girls "played the bear" in her rites at Brauron |
| Artemis Limnatis | Lakes and marshes |

### Aphrodite

| Epithet | Small thing she was for |
| --- | --- |
| Aphrodite Euploia | **Safe sea voyages** |
| Aphrodite Ambologera | **"Postponer of old age"** (at Sparta) |
| Aphrodite Apostrophia | **Turning away** unlawful desires (at Thebes) |

## Tiny household and everyday gods

| God / spirit | Small thing they were for |
| --- | --- |
| **Eunostos** | **Flour mills**. Her statue was set up at the mill to keep it running well |
| **Matton** | **Kneading dough** (a daimon honoured by Spartan cooks) |
| **Keraon** | **Mixing wine** (honoured alongside Matton) |
| **Deipneus** | **Bread-making** and meals (in Achaea) |
| **Akratopotes** | Drinking **unmixed wine** (honoured at Munychia) |
| **Kyamites** | **Beans**. A hero with a shrine on the Sacred Way near Athens |
| **Myiagros / Myiodes** | **Chasing away flies** (in Arcadia) |
| Hecate | Doorways, **keys** and entrances; food left at crossroads at the new moon |
| Hymenaios | The wedding song and wedding procession |
| Aristaeus | **Beekeeping**, **cheese-making** and olive-oil pressing |
| Priapus | Kitchen gardens and scaring off thieves and birds |
| Telesphoros | **Convalescence**, meaning getting better after an illness |
| Epione | **Soothing pain** |
| Pan | Sudden **panic**, echoes and the reed pipe |

### The kiln demons

A Homeric poem written for potters names the spirits who ruin a firing:

| Demon | What they do to the pottery |
| --- | --- |
| Syntribos | The Shatterer |
| Smaragos | The Smasher |
| Asbetos | The Unquenchable (the fire that burns too hot) |
| Sabaktes | The Destroyer |
| Omodamos | Crude-baker (leaves the pots underfired) |

## A note on "Athena, goddess of heirlooms"

I can't find a source that makes Athena the goddess of heirlooms. The nearest real matches are:

- **Zeus Ktesios**, guardian of the household's possessions and storeroom, which is the family's
  goods passed down in the home.
- **Athena Phratria / Zeus Phratrios**, guardians of family clans and lineage.

If you've seen "heirlooms" in a specific book or site, it may be a modern interpretation of one of these.
