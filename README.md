# Greek Gods Of…

A reference list of the Greek gods and what each one is the god or goddess *of*.
For example, **Athena** is the goddess of wisdom, warfare strategy and crafts.

Greek mythology has hundreds of named deities, spirits and personifications, so no list is ever truly
exhaustive. This one covers the Olympians, the primordial gods, the Titans and the most notable
other gods, grouped by family. Roman equivalents are given where they exist.

## The Twelve Olympians

| God | God/Goddess of | Roman name |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Zeus | The sky, thunder, lightning, law, order and justice; king of the gods | Jupiter |
| Hera | Marriage, women, childbirth and family; queen of the gods | Juno |
| Poseidon | The sea, earthquakes, storms and horses | Neptune |
| Demeter | The harvest, agriculture, grain and fertility of the earth | Ceres |
| Athena | Wisdom, warfare strategy, handicraft and reason | Minerva |
| Apollo | The sun, light, music, poetry, prophecy, healing and archery | Apollo |
| Artemis | The hunt, wilderness, wild animals, the moon and childbirth | Diana |
| Ares | War, violence, courage and bloodshed | Mars |
| Aphrodite | Love, beauty, desire and pleasure | Venus |
| Hephaestus | Fire, the forge, metalworking, blacksmiths and craftsmen | Vulcan |
| Hermes | Messengers, travel, trade, thieves, luck and guide of souls to the Underworld | Mercury |
| Dionysus | Wine, the grape harvest, festivity, theatre and ecstasy | Bacchus |

Hestia (goddess of the hearth, home and domestic life; Roman: Vesta) is sometimes counted as one of the
twelve in place of Dionysus.

Hades (god of the Underworld and the dead; Roman: Pluto) is one of the major gods but lives in the
Underworld rather than on Mount Olympus, so he is usually not counted among the Olympians.
